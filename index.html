How often should I use Tan Vanish Cream?

Once a week, preferably in the evening. Do not use it daily, or in both the morning and night.

How long do I leave it on?

After mixing, apply and wait 10–20 minutes. The pack turns from pink to white. Then rinse with water and apply the Barrier Protect Cream.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, before each use. Apply a little on your forearm and wait 24 hours. If irritation occurs, consult a doctor.

What's inside the kit?

Three parts: Gluta-Kojic Lotion, Gluta-Kojic Powder, and Barrier Protect Pre & Post Polish Cream.

Where should I not apply it?

Do not apply on beard, eyebrows or moustache. It contains lightening agents that may turn hair golden while removing tan. For external use only.
